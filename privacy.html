Mellow

Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Mellow has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings stay on your computer.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings, presets, schedule, sleep times and chosen place.
  • Your location, if you allow it. Mellow uses it only to work out sunrise and sunset, on your computer.

What leaves your computer

  • Update check, once a day (Mac). Mellow asks GitHub for the latest version number. GitHub sees your IP address, nothing else about you.
  • City search (Mac). When you type a city, the text goes to Apple to find it on the map.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads one animation library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Both see your IP address in their logs. Fonts come from this site itself. There are no cookies and no analytics.

Email

If you email us, we keep the conversation to reply to you. We don't add you to any list.

Your data

We don't sell or share anything. To have your data removed, or to ask what we have, email lowsunlabs@proton.me. To remove everything Mellow keeps, delete the app and its settings: on a Mac ~/Library/Preferences/com.inspiractus.mellow.plist, on Windows %APPDATA%\Mellow, on Linux ~/.config/mellow and ~/.local/state/mellow.
