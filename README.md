<h1 align="center">Mellow</h1>
<p align="center">Night Shift, but better. An app for Mac and Linux that warms, dims and softens your screen, and reminds you to take breaks.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/Inspiractus01/mellow-app/releases/latest/download/Mellow.dmg"><b>Mac</b></a> · <a href="https://inspiractus01.github.io/mellow-app/#download"><b>Linux</b></a> · <a href="https://inspiractus01.github.io/mellow-app/">Website</a></p>

- Warms the screen, dims it and softens colors
- Automatic mode that follows sunset and your sleep
- Reminds you to look away every 20 minutes

Free. Mac: macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. Linux: Hyprland (for example Omarchy).
The apps aren't signed yet, so the first time macOS asks you to allow Mellow in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
